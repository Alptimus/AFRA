# Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) — Benchmark Research Summary

## Company Profile
- **Ticker:** TSLA
- **Sector:** Consumer Discretionary
- **Industry:** Automobiles
- **CEO:** Elon Musk (since 2008)
- **Headquarters:** Austin, TX, USA
- **Employees:** ~140,000 (2024)
- **Market Cap:** ~$780B (Sep 2024)
- **Exchange:** NASDAQ

## Business Segments
1. **Automotive** — $82.4B (FY2023, includes regulatory credits)
2. **Energy Generation and Storage** — $6.0B (Megapack, Powerwall, solar)
3. **Services and Other** — $8.3B (FSD subscriptions, supercharging, insurance)

## Key Risk Factors (from 10-K)
- Margin compression from price cuts to defend market share
- Intense EV competition from BYD, legacy OEMs (Ford, GM, VW, Hyundai)
- CEO distraction / key-person risk (Elon Musk attention across SpaceX, X, xAI)
- Regulatory risk: NHTSA investigations on Autopilot/FSD safety
- China factory and supply chain dependence
- Execution risk on Cybertruck ramp, next-gen affordable model, Robotaxi
- Brand perception risk from CEO's public statements

## Competitive Position
- #2 global BEV manufacturer by volume (after BYD surpassed in H2 2023)
- #1 in North America BEV market (~50% share)
- Supercharger network — emerging as North American charging standard (NACS)
- Energy storage: #1 utility-scale battery deployments (Megapack)
- FSD/Autonomy: Most miles of real-world autonomy data, but no full L4 yet
- Manufacturing: Industry-leading Gigacasting and vertically integrated supply chain

## Recent Developments (2024)
- 2024 deliveries ~1.79M vehicles (slight decline from 1.81M in 2023)
- Robotaxi event (October 2024): Unveiled Cybercab, no steering wheel
- Gross margins stabilized at ~18% after aggressive price cuts in 2023
- Energy business growing 100%+ YoY (Megapack capacity expansion)
- FSD v12 (end-to-end neural network) launched — subscription $99/mo
- Laid off ~14,000 employees (10% of workforce) in April 2024
