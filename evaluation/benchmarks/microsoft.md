# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) — Benchmark Research Summary

## Company Profile
- **Ticker:** MSFT
- **Sector:** Technology
- **Industry:** Software — Infrastructure
- **CEO:** Satya Nadella (since 2014)
- **Headquarters:** Redmond, WA, USA
- **Employees:** ~228,000 (FY2024)
- **Market Cap:** ~$3.1 trillion (Sep 2024)
- **Exchange:** NASDAQ

## Business Segments
1. **Intelligent Cloud** (Azure, server products) — $96.0B revenue (FY2024)
2. **Productivity and Business Processes** (Office 365, LinkedIn, Dynamics) — $78.4B
3. **More Personal Computing** (Windows, Surface, Xbox, search) — $70.7B

## Key Risk Factors (from 10-K)
- Intense competition from AWS, Google Cloud in cloud computing
- Regulatory scrutiny (EU DMA, antitrust) on bundling practices
- AI investment uncertainty — massive capital expenditure on AI infrastructure
- Cybersecurity threats and reputation risk from security incidents
- Foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations (50%+ revenue international)

## Competitive Position
- #2 cloud provider globally (Azure ~25% market share vs AWS ~31%)
- Dominant in enterprise productivity (Office 365 >400M seats)
- AI integration leader via OpenAI partnership and Copilot products
- Gaming: #3 after acquisition of Activision Blizzard ($69B, closed Oct 2023)

## Recent Developments (2024)
- Azure growth ~30% YoY driven by AI workloads
- Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout generating ~$1B+ annualized revenue
- Massive data center capex ($55B+ planned for FY2025)
- GitHub Copilot >1.8M paid subscribers
